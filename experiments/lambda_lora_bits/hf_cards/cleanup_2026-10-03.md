## 3. Gaps found (things the paper uses that are not on the Hub; not acted on)

1. `analysis/output_vs_weights.json` (Table 4, "Student tokens r / markers r") and `analysis/seed_region_overlap.json` (6.2, "54–60%") exist locally in `experiments/` but were never uploaded. `upload_hf.py` only globs `experiments/*.json` at the time of the first upload.
2. Unedited-adapter MATH-500 records that Tables 1–2 and every section-5 base use are missing from the Hub. Upload them before deleting the stale copies:
   - `{sweep,sweep_llama}/eval_math500_7680/` (18 files each, about 41 MB and 46 MB locally), for A/Qwen and A/Llama
   - `pairD/olmo1b/eval_math500/lambda*_seed0.json` (6 files, about 6.6 MB locally, 200×2), for C/OLMo seed 0
   
   All other kept dataset files (1,638 checked, GPQA samples excluded since they are stripped by design) match the local sources in size. All 1,026 kept adapters match the local `runs*/…/adapter/` files in size.
3. `analysis/reproducibility.lock.json` does not pin the paper's pair C endpoints (DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Llama-8B, Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct). It does pin unused models (Llama-3.2-3B, Qwen2.5-1.5B/3B, SmolLM2). The current model card claims all endpoint commits are pinned there. The new draft does not repeat that claim. The `interpolation.json` files under local `pairD/teachers/lambda*/` may have what is needed to add them.
4. The generations behind `analysis/graft_beta.json` (`<cell>/eval_graft_beta/`, β=2/4/8) and its λ=0 reference (`eval_ablation_loo/lambda0.0_base.json`) are not on the Hub. The β=4/8 exclusion therefore rests on the adapters plus that one JSON.
5. Naming: the paper's pair C is `pairD` in both repos. Renaming would need a re-upload (out of scope), so both READMEs carry the mapping table.
6. Current model card errors that the new drafts fix:
   - It lists 6 base models, including SmolLM2. That is why the Hub page shows `base_model: HuggingFaceTB/SmolLM2-1.7B-Instruct`.
   - It says every edit carries `ablation.json`. graft*/amp* do not.
   - The `adapter_config.json` files point `base_model_name_or_path` to a local `models/...` path. The new card says to load the base by Hub id.
