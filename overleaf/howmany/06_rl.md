# 6. Does Reinforcement Learning Write Less? — 문장 분해

출처: arXiv 2607.21351v1, `sections/6_rl.tex` (그림·표 캡션 제외)

## 문단 1 — 질문

- (배경: 검증되지 않은 주장)
   - The thirteen-parameter result of Morris et al. (2026a) rests on a claim nobody has measured: that supervised fine-tuning must absorb the whole token stream while reinforcement learning takes in only the reward, a few bits per prompt.
- (방법적 근거)
   - Our behavior-write bits are defined on the final policy alone (Eq. 2), so for once the two can be weighed on the same scale.

## 문단 2 — 정확도를 맞춘 비교

- (설정)
   - We hold the base and adapter fixed—Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct, LoRA rank 4—and train the two arms to the same accuracy on 3-digit arithmetic and key-value extraction, supervised on gold answers, GRPO (Shao et al. 2024) on a verifiable reward (Table 2).
- (결과: SFT)
   - In the matched bin the supervised adapter carries roughly a hundred excess bits and climbs from there—by 800 steps it has written nearly three times as much, quietly compressing a training set it already answers correctly.
- (결과: GRPO)
   - The GRPO adapter, at the same accuracy, carries nothing: its excess is zero to within the estimator's noise, and stays there across binary, graded, and noisy rewards alike.
- (요약)
   - Supervised fine-tuning keeps writing after it has learned the task; reinforcement learning stops.

## 문단 3 — 비밀 저장 실험

- (실험 도입)
   - The sharpest form of the difference is a secret nobody can reach except from memory.
- (설계)
   - We plant records whose answer—an eight-digit code—appears only as a supervised target, never in any prompt, so at test time it can come only from the weights.
- (결과: SFT)
   - Supervised fine-tuning takes them in wholesale: repeated secrets reach rank-1 extraction among ten thousand candidates, and even a once-seen secret lands within a few bits of certainty.
- (결과: GRPO)
   - GRPO, trained on the identical data, leaves every one of them at chance.
- (추가 분석)
   - Excluding the planted records from the accounting drops the supervised adapter's excess from roughly five thousand bits to essentially nothing—its entire measured surplus is the secrets, not a general habit of memorizing the task.
- (기제)
   - The mechanism is plain: a verifiable reward pays out only when the policy happens to produce the exact secret, which the base never will, so no gradient ever points toward it, while supervision writes it down by construction.
- (반론 차단: 학습 가능한 비밀)
   - And the asymmetry is not merely the impossibility of that gradient—made the secret learnable, a two-digit code a handful of samples can stumble onto, GRPO learns the task to 90% accuracy and still declines to store the secret that supervision copies down.

## 문단 4 — 기존 논쟁에 대한 함의

- (연결)
   - This bears directly on the elicitation-versus-injection debate.
- (논증)
   - If reinforcement learning were quietly injecting new content into the weights, a secret it was trained to reproduce is exactly the content that would show up—and it does not.
   - What the reward can do is raise or lower the probability of behavior the base can already produce; what it cannot do is deposit a fact the base would never sample on its own.
   - Supervised fine-tuning has no such limit, and writes the fact down the first time it sees it.
- (결론과 범위)
   - The bit-level picture is thus consistent with the elicitation reading of RLVR and hard to reconcile with strong injection, at least for the kind of content memorization would carry.

## 문단 5 — 한계

- (한계 예고)
   - Two honest edges.
- (한계 1: 정확도 불일치)
   - The secret test is a stress test, not an accuracy-matched comparison—supervised fine-tuning reaches perfect accuracy where GRPO spans the high nineties—so it shows what each method does with a memorable target, not a controlled bit rate.
- (한계 2: 예측이 맞지 않은 부분)
   - And the reward-entropy dial the conjecture predicts would move things, does not: richer rewards do not measurably raise what GRPO writes, because on these tasks it operates so far below any entropy bound that the bound is slack.
- (정리)
   - What the measurements do settle is the direction the debate has been arguing from behavior alone—reinforcement learning, at least here, deposits into the weights almost nothing of what it is shown.

## 구조 메모

- 제목이 질문형이고, 첫 문장이 "nobody has measured"로 갭을 바로 세움
- 두 실험을 약한 비교(정확도 맞춤) → 강한 비교(비밀 저장) 순서로 배치함
- 결과마다 기제 설명과 반론 차단(학습 가능한 두 자리 비밀)을 붙임
- 논쟁에 대한 결론은 "consistent with … hard to reconcile with …, at least for …"처럼 범위를 한정해 씀
- 한계를 "Two honest edges."로 본문 안에 따로 두고, 예측이 빗나간 결과(reward entropy)도 숨기지 않음
