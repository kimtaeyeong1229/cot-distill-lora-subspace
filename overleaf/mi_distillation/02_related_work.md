# 2. Related Work — 문장 분해

출처: arXiv 2608.29623, `acl_latex.tex`

## 2.1 Reasoning in Large Language Models

### 문단 1 — CoT와 Long CoT의 등장

- (배경)
   - Chain-of-thought (CoT) reasoning has become a key mechanism for enhancing the reasoning capabilities of large language models (Wang et al.; Jaech et al. 2024).
- (역사: Short CoT)
   - Early distillation paradigms typically rely on Short CoTs, either manually annotated or generated by instruction-tuned models, to provide concise intermediate reasoning steps.
- (역사: Long CoT)
   - More recently, reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards (RLVR) (Shao et al. 2024; Yu et al. 2025) has enabled language models to produce Long CoTs with detailed reflection, and verification, substantially improving performance on complex mathematical and symbolic reasoning tasks (He et al. 2024; Xu et al. 2024).

### 문단 2 — Long CoT의 한계

- (문제 제기)
   - However, Long CoT trajectories are not universally beneficial as distillation targets.
- (원인 가설)
   - Their rich reasoning patterns may also increase imitation difficulty for smaller students, particularly when the teacher rationale is poorly aligned with the student's learning capacity and intrinsic distribution.
- (동기)
   - This motivates reasoning supervision that balances informativeness with student-side learnability.

## 2.2 Knowledge Distillation

### 문단 1 — KD와 Long CoT 증류의 어려움

- (배경)
   - Knowledge distillation (Hinton et al. 2015) transfers the capabilities of large language models to smaller students by training them on outputs from stronger teachers.
- (문제 현상)
   - However, recent studies suggest that directly distilling Long CoT trajectories into small models does not consistently improve performance, as small students often benefit more from shorter and more concise rationales (Li et al. 2025; Kim et al. 2025; Jiang et al. 2025).

### 문단 2 — 기존 해법과 본 연구의 위치

- (선행 연구)
   - Existing approaches address this challenge by mixing short and long CoT data or introducing curriculum-based distillation, gradually increasing the complexity of the supervision (Jiang et al. 2025; Li et al. 2025).
- (갭)
   - Despite their effectiveness, these methods largely depend on manually designed data mixtures or schedules, and offer limited control over which reasoning traces are most suitable for a given student model.
- (본 연구의 위치)
   - Our work instead analyzes the scale-dependent effects of short and long CoT distillation, and constructs a continuous reasoning-data spectrum via model interpolation, enabling more adaptive selection of CoT trajectories for small-model distillation.
