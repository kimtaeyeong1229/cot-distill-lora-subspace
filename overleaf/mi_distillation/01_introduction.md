## 문단 1 — LRM과 Long CoT의 가치

- (배경)
   - Recent advances in large reasoning models (LRMs), including DeepSeek-R1 (Guo et al. 2025), Seed1.5-Thinking (ByteDance Seed 2025), and Kimi K2.5 (Kimi Team 2026), have demonstrated strong capabilities across complex problem-solving and code-generation tasks (He et al. 2024; AIME 2025; Jain et al. 2025).
   - Trained with reinforcement learning from verifiable rewards (RLVR) (Shao et al. 2024), these models exhibit strong test-time reasoning capabilities, and their Long CoT trajectories have further emerged as a valuable source of supervision for supervised fine-tuning (SFT) and cold-start reinforcement learning (Muennighoff et al. 2025; Guo et al. 2025).

## 문단 2 — 작은 모델로 옮길 때의 문제

- (동기)
   - However, the high computational cost of LRMs limits their deployment in resource-constrained scenarios (Lan et al. 2026; Feng et al. 2025), making it essential to transfer their reasoning abilities to smaller models.
- (자연스러운 접근)
   - A natural approach is to fine-tune small models directly on Long CoT traces generated by LRMs, with the expectation that richer reasoning supervision will lead to stronger reasoning performance (Yang et al. 2026; Just et al. 2025).
- (문제 현상)
   - Recent evidence shows that small models often fail to benefit from direct Long CoT supervision and may instead achieve better performance when trained on Short CoT data (Li et al. 2025; Kim et al. 2025), as shown in Figure 1.
- (시사점)
   - This highlights the importance of matching reasoning data to the student model in effective distillation.

## 문단 3 — 그래디언트 분석과 원리

- (연구 방법)
   - Motivated by these observations, we investigate the gradient signatures induced by Long and Short CoT across student models of varying scales.
- (분석 결과)
   - Our analysis shows that, compared with Short CoT, Long CoT induces gradients with larger magnitudes and more concentrated directions, and this effect becomes more pronounced as model scale increases.
- (해석)
   - These findings suggest that Long CoT supervision is effective when the student has sufficient capacity to translate verbose reasoning trajectories into coherent and low-dimensional parameter updates.
- (핵심 과제)
   - This highlights a key challenge in reasoning distillation: the supervision signal must be not only informative, but also compatible with the student's intrinsic distribution.

## 문단 4 — 기존 방법의 한계와 연구 질문

- (선행 연구)
   - Prior work has sought to narrow this gap by mixing Long and Short CoT data (Li et al. 2025) or by adopting curriculum distillation strategies (Jiang et al. 2025) that gradually reduce the learning difficulty.
- (갭)
   - However, such strategies provide only coarse control over the supervision signal, since the mixing ratios or curriculum schedules are usually manually designed and the resulting trajectories remain limited in diversity.
- (연구 질문)
   - This raises two central questions: (1) How can we construct more fine-grained reasoning trajectories that better match students of different capacities, and (2) How can we select trajectories that are both informative and well aligned with the student model?

## 문단 5 — 제안 방법

- (방법 1: 스펙트럼)
   - In this work, we propose MI-Distillation, a CoT distillation framework that constructs a continuous Instruct-Reasoning data spectrum through model interpolation.
   - By interpolating between instruction-oriented and reasoning-oriented models, our framework generates reasoning trajectories with varying length, correctness, and reasoning depth.
   - We show that this spectrum changes systematically with the interpolation coefficient λ, revealing controllable variations in trajectory quality and reasoning length.
- (방법 2: 선택 기준)
   - To select suitable supervision from this spectrum, we introduce SeqLSS (Sequential Learnable Surprisal Score), a Long CoT selection criterion that balances reasoning information density with student-induced distributional shift.
- (기대 효과)
   - This allows small student models to learn from trajectories that are both informative and well aligned with their intrinsic capabilities.

## 문단 6 — 기여

- (기여 도입)
   - To summarize, our main contributions are as follows:
- (기여 목록)
   - We analyze the gradient dynamics of Long and Short CoT distillation, explaining both the difficulty of Long CoT distillation for small models and the role of model scale in reasoning transfer.
   - We propose MI-Distillation, which builds a fine-grained Instruct-Reasoning data spectrum via model interpolation, and introduce SeqLSS for selecting student-aligned reasoning trajectories.
   - Extensive experiments on challenging reasoning benchmarks show that MI-Distillation consistently improves small model reasoning and surpasses strong CoT distillation baselines.
