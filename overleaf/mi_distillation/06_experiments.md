## Experiment Details.

- (목적)
   - To evaluate the effectiveness and robustness of MI-Distillation, we conduct experiments on two student models from different model families: Qwen2.5-3B-Instruct and Llama3.2-3B-Instruct.
- (teacher)
   - We use QwQ-32B as the Long CoT teacher and Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct as the Short CoT teacher.
- (생성 설정)
   - For data generation, we follow the recommended decoding setting with temperature 0.6, top-p 0.95 (Guo et al. 2025), and a maximum generation length of 8192 tokens.
- (데이터)
   - The training corpus is constructed from 7,500 randomly sampled problems from the MATH training set (Hendrycks et al. 2021).
   - For each problem, we generate candidate reasoning trajectories using the two endpoint teachers as well as the interpolated teachers in our MI-Distillation framework.
- (하이퍼파라미터)
   - Unless otherwise specified, the learnability penalty coefficient α is empirically set to 4.

## Training and Evaluation.

- (학습 설정)
   - All student models are fine-tuned for 3 epochs using LLaMA-Factory (Zheng et al. 2024), with a global batch size of 32 and a peak learning rate of 1×10^-5.
- (평가 벤치마크)
   - To comprehensively assess the distilled students, we evaluate them on a suite of reasoning benchmarks with varying difficulty, including GSM8K (Cobbe et al. 2021), MATH-500 (Hendrycks et al. 2021), AMC23 (AMC 2025), AIME24 (AIME 2024), GPQA-Diamond (Rein et al. 2023), Minerva (Lewkowycz et al. 2022), and OlympiadBench (He et al. 2024).
- (평가 지표)
   - We follow the teacher-generation decoding protocol and report Pass@1 with the corresponding standard deviation.
- (부록 안내)
   - Further details regarding hardware configurations and inference setups are provided in Appendix C.

## Baselines.

- (비교 대상 개요)
   - In addition to standard distillation from Short CoT and Long CoT teachers, we compare MI-Distillation against three stronger baselines that combine or schedule different types of CoT supervision.
- (baseline 1)
   - Mix Long (Li et al. 2025) randomly mixes Short-CoT and Long-CoT trajectories at a 4:1 ratio, providing a simple data-level combination of concise and elaborated reasoning traces.
- (baseline 2)
   - Mix Large (Li et al. 2025) extends this setting by incorporating trajectories generated from teachers of different scales under the same mixing ratio, thereby increasing teacher diversity.
- (baseline 3)
   - Curriculum Learning (Jiang et al. 2025) introduces Long-CoT supervision progressively during training, encouraging the student to gradually adapt from simpler reasoning patterns to more complex ones.

## 6.1 Results and Analysis

### Main Results.

- (결과)
   - Table 2 reports the main comparison on two 3B student models.
   - MI-Distillation achieves the best average performance on both Qwen2.5-3B-Instruct and Llama3.2-3B-Instruct, outperforming the strongest baseline by 1.12 and 1.40 points, respectively.
   - It also yields consistent gains on most challenging reasoning benchmarks, including AMC23, MATH-500, and OlympiadBench.
- (앞선 분석과 연결)
   - Consistent with our analysis, Long CoT distillation often underperforms Short CoT distillation, showing that more detailed rationales are not inherently more learnable for small students.
- (해석)
   - By selecting trajectories from the Instruct-Reasoning spectrum, MI-Distillation better balances reasoning depth and student learnability, leading to more robust improvements across model families.

### Comparison with Fixed Interpolation Coefficients.

- (비교 설정)
   - Table 3 compares MI-Distillation with direct distillation from CoT trajectories generated under fixed interpolation coefficients.
- (결과)
   - MI-Distillation consistently achieves the best performance across GSM8K, MATH-500, and AMC23, outperforming the strongest fixed-λ baseline by 0.19, 2.70, and 1.09 points, respectively.
- (핵심 관찰)
   - Crucially, no single interpolation coefficient dominates across all benchmarks: λ=0.8 performs best on GSM8K, whereas λ=0.4 and λ=0.6 are stronger on MATH-500 and AMC23.
- (해석)
   - This highlights the limitation of using a globally fixed interpolation coefficient for reasoning distillation.
   - In contrast, MI-Distillation adaptively selects trajectories from the Instruct-Reasoning spectrum, enabling the student to benefit from reasoning traces that better balance information density with learnability.

### Ablation on the SeqLSS Selection Criterion.

- (목적)
   - To verify that the gains of MI-Distillation stem from the proposed ranking criterion itself, we compare SeqLSS against widely used data-selection baselines on the same candidate trajectories generated by model interpolation, including perplexity-based selection (PPL) (Marion et al. 2023), IFD (Li et al. 2024), and random selection.
- (반대 조건)
   - We additionally include SeqLSS-min, which instead selects the trajectories with the lowest SeqLSS scores, to examine whether the proposed ranking identifies genuinely useful supervision.
- (결과)
   - As shown in Table 4, SeqLSS achieves the best average performance and outperforms all baselines on four of the five benchmarks.
   - In contrast, SeqLSS-min performs worst overall, confirming that the improvement is attributable to the proposed ranking criterion rather than merely to subsampling the candidate pool.
- (부록 안내)
   - A complementary sensitivity analysis of the learnability penalty coefficient α is provided in Appendix D.5.

### Training Dynamics under Different Learnability Penalties.

- (목적)
   - We further examine the training dynamics of student models under different strengths of the learnability penalty in MI-Distillation.
- (설정)
   - Figure 4 plots the training loss of Qwen2.5-3B-Instruct and Llama-3.2-3B-Instruct when varying the penalty coefficient α.
- (결과 1)
   - Across both student backbones, MI-Distillation exhibits substantially lower and more stable training loss than Long CoT and Mix Long, indicating that selecting trajectories with explicit learnability control alleviates the optimization difficulty introduced by overly complex reasoning traces.
- (결과 2)
   - Meanwhile, increasing α generally leads to smoother optimization and lower loss, especially for Llama-3.2-3B-Instruct, suggesting that stronger learnability-aware selection can better align the distilled trajectories with the student model's learning capacity.
- (반론 차단)
   - Although Short CoT achieves the lowest training loss, it provides less informative reasoning supervision.
   - MI-Distillation maintains a favorable balance by preserving richer reasoning signals while remaining more learnable than direct Long-CoT distillation.
