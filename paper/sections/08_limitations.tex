\section*{Limitations}

We study only off-policy distillation measured with LoRA, with students of at most 1B parameters and MATH as the only training task, and model family and scale change together across the teacher pairs. We do not know whether the same region arises under full fine-tuning, on-policy distillation, or larger students, and the only evidence from outside math is GPQA-Diamond.

We measure thinking by the frequency of reflective markers, and we re-examine it with internal features only for Llama. Those features are also selected by a textual contrast between teacher solutions, so they may capture writing style rather than the way of thinking.

The result that amplification does not increase thinking cannot fully rule out a ceiling effect, and on the grafting side we did not find what is needed to finish a solution.

We separate thinking from length only at generation time, through a length-matched cut; we have no control that trains on short solutions of the same length.

Future work could proceed in three directions. First, find what is needed to finish a solution and check whether transferring it together with the shared region also transfers the solution. Second, test whether the same region arises under full fine-tuning, on-policy distillation, and larger students. Third, add a control trained on short solutions of the same length, so that thinking and length are also separated at training time.
